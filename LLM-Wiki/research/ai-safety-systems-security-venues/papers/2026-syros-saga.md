# tle: "SAGA: A Security Architecture for Governing AI Agentic Systems

## Role and method

SAGA uses a central Provider for agent registration, user-defined policy, and cryptographic access tokens controlling inter-agent communication. The official abstract reports low overhead across local and cloud models.

## Boundary

A governance control plane; a content detector may supply risk signals but should not be the sole authorization mechanism.
