# tle: "I know what you MEME! Understanding and Detecting Harmful Memes with Multimodal Large Language Models

## Role and method

HMGUARD combines adaptive prompting with structured multimodal reasoning after analyzing harmful memes through composition and propaganda techniques. The paper reports 0.92 accuracy and 0.91 F1 on public data, and 0.88 accuracy and 0.86 F1 in a real-world setting.

## Boundary

Direct multimodal moderation evidence, but no common lightweight-throughput baseline.
