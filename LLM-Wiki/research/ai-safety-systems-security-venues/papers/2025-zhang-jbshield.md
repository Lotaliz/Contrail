# tle: "JBShield: Defending Large Language Models from Jailbreak Attacks through Activated Concept Analysis and Manipulation

## Role and method

JBShield detects jailbreak-related activated concepts and modifies hidden representations after detection. The official page reports 0.95 average detection accuracy and a reduction of average ASR from 61% to 2%.

## Boundary

Shows the value of internal signals, but requires white-box model access.
