# tle: "ACE: A Security Architecture for LLM-Integrated App Systems

## Role and method

ACE separates a trusted abstract plan from app-bound concrete planning, statically verifies structured plans for information-flow constraints, and enforces data and capability barriers during execution.

## Boundary

A true system-security design: probabilistic planning is surrounded by deterministic verification and enforcement.
