# tle: "Benchmarking and Understanding Safety Risks in AI Character Platforms

## Role and method

The study evaluates 16 AI-character platforms with 5,000 questions across 16 safety categories. It reports 65.1% average unsafe response rate versus 17.7% for baselines and an F1 of 0.81 for predicting less-safe characters.

## Boundary

Platform personas and product layers can dominate base-model safety; continuous calibration is required.
