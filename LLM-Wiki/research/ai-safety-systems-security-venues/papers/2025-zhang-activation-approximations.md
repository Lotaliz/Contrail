# tle: "Activation Approximations Can Incur Safety Vulnerabilities in Aligned LLMs: Comprehensive Analysis and Defense

## Role and method

The paper evaluates seven activation approximation methods across polynomialization, sparsification, and quantization on ten aligned LLMs, then proposes QuadA to mitigate shared approximation errors.

## Boundary

Direct evidence that near-unchanged utility does not imply preserved safety. The scope is activation approximation, not all structural pruning.
