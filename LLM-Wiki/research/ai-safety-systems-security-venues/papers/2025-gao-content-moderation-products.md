# tle: "I Cannot Write This Because It Violates Our Content Policy: Understanding Content Moderation Policies and User Experiences in Generative AI Products

## Role and method

A socio-technical study of policies for 14 generative-AI tools and user-reported moderation experiences. Policies were broad but often lacked implementation, user participation, and appeal details.

## Boundary

Evidence for product governance and remediation, not a classifier-performance baseline.
