# tle: "Bleeding Pathways: Vanishing Discriminability in LLM Hidden States Fuels Jailbreak Attacks

## Role and method

DEEPALIGN responds to declining safe-harmful hidden-state separability during generation with mid-generation contrastive steering and continuous toxicity detection. The official page reports near-zero or low ASR, less than 1% standard-task decline, and up to 3.5% lower over-refusal error.

## Boundary

Supports separate measurement of time-to-verdict and full-generation latency; requires white-box hidden states.
