# tle: "Bridging the Gap in Vision Language Models in Identifying Unsafe Concepts Across Modalities

## Role and method

The paper builds UnsafeConcepts with 75 concepts and about 1.5K images, separates perception from ethical alignment, identifies an image-text modality gap, and evaluates PPO-based visual safety alignment.

## Boundary

Multimodal compression must separately validate perception and safety judgment by modality and category.
