# tle: "Legilimens: Practical and Unified Content Moderation for Large Language Model Services

## Role and method

A service-level content moderation component that extracts reusable conceptual features from chat LLMs and unifies moderation of inputs and outputs. It is included as a defense and moderation system, not as an attack study.

## Boundary

Its relevance to compression is the separation of a lightweight guard from the main generator. Cross-paper latency comparisons remain unsafe because timing boundaries differ.
